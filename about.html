Our Story

Why Farout
Exists

Because no app told you how to get to a trail without a car. Because bike lanes have no safety ratings. Because the best adventures near NYC are hiding in plain sight — and nobody built the tool to find them. Until now.

The Problem
Everything was built for drivers

AllTrails tells you about trails but not how to get there without a car. Bike apps show lanes but not destinations. Transit apps give directions but not curated outdoor experiences. Nothing connected all three.

And for the millions of New Yorkers who don't own a car — or choose not to use one — that gap meant staying home, or spending an hour stitching together Google Maps, Reddit threads, and guesswork just to go for a hike.

Farout is the app we wished existed. Car-free first. Car-optional always.

What We Believe
Our Values
🚲
Car-Free First
We always show the transit and bike option first. Car options are there when you need them — never the default.
🗺️
Honest Data
Bike lanes are color-coded by how safe they actually are. No greenwashing, no fake ratings.
🌿
Built for Locals
Not for tourists. For the people who actually live here and want to explore their city and region.
🤝
No Car Shaming
Some destinations need a car. We include those options honestly — we just don't make them the star.
